# Data

| Folder | Contents | Real or simulated |
|---|---|---|
| `raw/` | `pak_ADM2.geojson` (districts) and `pak_ADM1.geojson` (provinces) from geoBoundaries, public domain. `census_2023_district_population.csv`, built from the Pakistan Population Census 2023 by `scripts/prepare_census_population.py` | **Public** |
| `simulated/` | `retailers.csv`, `retail_census.csv`, `district_attributes.csv`, generated by `src/footprint/simulate.py` with a fixed random seed | **Simulated. Not real company data.** |
| `processed/` | `district_model.csv` (one row per district with every metric and result). `retailers_mapped.csv` is rebuilt by `run_pipeline.py` | Derived from the above |

## Simulated files

**retailers.csv**: one row per retailer that has (or had) a freezer.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `retailer_id` | Simulated identifier |
| `region`, `distributor_id`, `channel` | Sales region, distributor and trade channel |
| `district_typed` | District name as typed by the sales team (deliberately messy) |
| `latitude`, `longitude` | GPS coordinates (a few are deliberately swapped or missing) |
| `induction_year` | Year the retailer received a freezer |
| `status` | Active, or the year it closed |
| `volume_2022` to `volume_2024` | Liters sold in each year |

**retail_census.csv**: stores listed by a (simulated) third party retail census.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `store_id`, `latitude`, `longitude` | Simulated store and its location |
| `channel` | Grocery, General Store, Bakery, Supermarket, Petro Mart, Pharmacy, Tea Shop, Restaurant |
| `turnover_band`, `est_monthly_turnover_pkr` | Turnover level and estimate |
| `sells_coke_or_pepsi`, `sells_dairy_milk`, `sells_lays`, `sells_sooper` | Whether the store sells each competitor brand |

**district_attributes.csv**: sales region, LSM 5+ share (Living Standards Measure, the target population share) and nature of district (Stronghold, Neighbouring, Population High, Rest).
